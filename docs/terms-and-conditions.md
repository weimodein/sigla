## 2. Suggest a Word Feature

The Suggest a Word feature allows registered users to contribute new Filipino Sign Language (FSL) gesture data to the system. By using this feature, you agree to the following rules:

- You may only suggest **words that do not already exist** in the system. If a word is already available, your submission will be blocked and you will be returned to the submission form.
- Once a word is accepted and gesture samples are collected, **no additional samples may be submitted** for that word — by any user or by the administrator. This ensures consistent sample quality across all words.
- All gesture samples submitted are **final**. You may not edit or replace submitted samples.
- Submissions must represent **legitimate FSL gestures**. Submitting random, inappropriate, or intentionally incorrect gestures is a violation of these terms.
- Submitted gesture data — including hand landmark coordinates extracted from your recordings — will be used to **train and improve the SigLa gesture recognition model**. By submitting, you grant SigLa a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use your contribution for this purpose.

## 3. Gesture and Video Data Collection

When using the Suggest a Word feature, the application captures hand gesture data from your device's camera. Specifically:

- Video frames of your hand gestures are processed **on-device** to extract **hand landmark coordinates** (126 data points per frame)
- These coordinates — not the raw video — are what is stored and submitted to the system
- Raw video footage is not permanently stored on our servers

By using the Suggest a Word feature, you consent to this data collection for the purpose of building and improving the sign language recognition system.
